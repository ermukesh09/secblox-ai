**To choose a SOC 2 auditor, start with the non-negotiable — a licensed CPA firm that performs SOC 2 engagements under AICPA standards — then compare experience with companies like yours, timeline, how fieldwork runs and scope-based pricing.** The auditor you pick shapes how smooth the audit feels and how buyers receive your report, so it's worth an hour of structured comparison.

## What are the must-haves for a SOC 2 auditor?

- **A licensed CPA firm.** Only CPA firms can perform SOC 2 examinations and issue the report.
- **SOC 2 experience.** The firm should perform SOC engagements regularly, not occasionally.
- **Peer review.** CPA firms performing attest work are subject to AICPA peer review; ask for their most recent result.
- **Independence.** The firm must be independent from you — it generally can't also design and implement your controls.

If a firm fails any of these, stop there.

## What else should a startup compare?

| Factor | Why it matters | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Experience with your stack | Faster walkthroughs, fewer misunderstandings | Regular cloud-native SaaS clients on AWS, Azure or GCP |
| Company size fit | Enterprise-focused firms may be slow or costly for startups | Many clients at your stage |
| Timeline and availability | Deals may be waiting on the report | Committed fieldwork and report dates |
| How fieldwork runs | Determines how much of your team's time it takes | Clear request lists, organised walkthroughs |
| Evidence formats | Avoids re-doing evidence | Accepts exports from your existing tools |
| Pricing model | Prevents surprise fees | Fixed, scope-based quote |
| Report recognition | Buyers must accept it | Reports routinely accepted by enterprise customers in your market |

## What questions should you ask a SOC 2 auditor?

1. How many SOC 2 audits do you complete each year for SaaS companies our size?
2. Who will actually perform fieldwork, and how experienced are they?
3. What's included in the quote, and what would trigger extra fees?
4. How do you handle exceptions and management responses?
5. What evidence formats do you accept from our existing tools?
6. When can you start fieldwork, and when will we receive the report?
7. Can we see a redacted sample report?
8. Do you also offer readiness services? (If yes, confirm how independence is kept.)

## How should you compare SOC 2 audit quotes?

Send every firm the same written scope: Trust Services Criteria, Type I or Type II, observation window, systems in scope, headcount and locations. Quotes based on different assumptions can't be compared. The audit fee is only one part of the total — see [SOC 2 cost for startups](/blog/soc-2-cost-for-startups/) for the full budget.

## What are the red flags?

- A guaranteed "clean report" before they've seen your environment
- No CPA license, or a non-CPA company issuing the report itself
- An unusually low price with vague scope
- Fieldwork timing they won't commit to
- Pressure to also buy their own implementation services without addressing independence

## When should you choose the auditor?

Choose the auditor once you know your scope — usually during readiness, a few months before you want the report. For a Type II, agree the observation window start date with them so it's on record. See the [SOC 2 audit process](/blog/soc-2-audit-process/) for the full sequence.

## In practice

The cheapest audit quote isn't always the cheapest audit. What drives total effort is how clearly the firm scopes the work and how organised its fieldwork is: clear request lists and committed dates save your engineers far more time than a small difference in fee. We also see value in firms that accept evidence exported from the tools you already use, rather than asking for everything in a new format.

## FAQ

### Can my SOC 2 consultant also be my auditor?

Generally no. Independence rules mean the firm that audits you shouldn't have designed or implemented your controls. Many companies use a consultant for readiness and a separate CPA firm for the audit.

### Does the size of the audit firm matter?

Not for validity — any qualified CPA firm's SOC 2 report is valid. Size matters for fit: price, availability and how buyers in your market perceive the report.

### Should we switch auditors every year?

There's no requirement to. Staying with a firm that knows your environment usually makes renewals smoother; switch if service, price or report acceptance becomes a problem.

### Can a compliance platform choose an auditor for us?

Many platforms have auditor partnerships and can introduce firms, but the decision and the engagement are yours. Compare those firms like any other. See [SOC 2 consultant vs compliance platform](/blog/soc-2-consultant-vs-compliance-platform/).
