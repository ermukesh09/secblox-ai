**SOC 2 proves your security controls work; ISO/IEC 42001 proves your AI is governed. Most AI companies selling to US enterprises should get SOC 2 first, then add ISO 42001 when customers or regulators ask for formal AI governance assurance.** Here is how they differ, where they overlap and how to sequence them without doubling the work.

## Which should an AI company get first?

**Start with SOC 2** if US enterprise buyers are blocking deals on security. **Add ISO 42001** when customers, partners or regulators want formal assurance that your AI is governed — or when you sell into markets where ISO certifications carry more weight than AICPA reports.

## How do SOC 2 and ISO 42001 differ?

| Aspect | SOC 2 | ISO/IEC 42001 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | Security of customer data and systems | Responsible management of AI systems |
| Issued by | Licensed CPA firm (attestation report) | Accredited certification body (certificate) |
| Structure | Trust Services Criteria | Management system clauses 4–10 + Annex A controls |
| Validity | Report covers a period; renewed annually | Certificate cycle with surveillance audits |
| Strongest in | US B2B procurement | Global, AI-specific assurance |

## Where do SOC 2 and ISO 42001 overlap?

Risk assessment, access control, change management, vendor management, incident response, logging, internal audit and management oversight appear in both. A mature SOC 2 program covers a meaningful share of the operational groundwork ISO 42001 expects.

## What does ISO 42001 add beyond SOC 2?

- AI policy and AI-specific roles
- AI system impact assessments on individuals and society
- Data quality, provenance and AI lifecycle controls
- Transparency and human-oversight requirements
- A management-system cycle of internal audit, management review and continual improvement

## What is the recommended sequence?

1. Scope SOC 2 with AI components explicitly inside the boundary.
2. Build an AI inventory and run a NIST AI RMF assessment in parallel.
3. Extend shared controls, then add ISO 42001-specific processes.
4. Pursue certification once your AIMS has run long enough to show internal audit and management review.

## FAQ

### Does SOC 2 cover AI governance?

Only partly. SOC 2 covers the security of systems that include AI, but not AI-specific topics such as impact assessments, transparency or human oversight. That is what ISO 42001 adds.

### Can one audit firm do both?

SOC 2 reports are issued by licensed CPA firms; ISO 42001 certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies. Some firms offer both through separate teams, but they remain two separate audits.
